8. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER GDPR

As a European resident, you have these rights:

RIGHT TO ACCESS (Art. 15)
   What: Get a copy of all your personal data
   How: Contact privacy@travelrust.com with "Data Access Request"
   Timeline: 30 days
   Cost: Free for first request per year
   In app: Settings > Privacy > Export My Data

RIGHT TO RECTIFICATION (Art. 16)
   What: Correct inaccurate data
   How: Edit in app settings, or contact us
   Timeline: Without undue delay
   Cost: Free

RIGHT TO ERASURE / RIGHT TO BE FORGOTTEN (Art. 17)
   What: Delete your account and personal data
   How: Settings > Account > Delete Account
   Timeline: 30 days (payment records kept 10 years)
   Cost: Free
   Important: Some data kept for legal compliance

RIGHT TO RESTRICT PROCESSING (Art. 18)
   What: Stop us from using your data (while disputing)
   How: Contact privacy@travelrust.com
   Timeline: Without undue delay
   Cost: Free

RIGHT TO DATA PORTABILITY (Art. 20)
   What: Get your data in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)
   How: Settings > Privacy > Export Data, or email
   Timeline: 30 days
   Cost: Free
   Format: JSON or CSV available

RIGHT TO OBJECT (Art. 21)
   What: Opt-out of non-essential processing
   How: Settings > Privacy > Opt-out options
   Timeline: Immediate for optional processing
   Cost: Free
   Includes: Analytics, marketing emails, profiling

RIGHT TO WITHDRAW CONSENT (Art. 7)
   What: Stop optional data collection
   How: Settings > Privacy & Tracking
   Timeline: Immediate
   Cost: Free
   Service: Still works (non-consent affects only optional features)

RIGHTS RELATED TO AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING (Art. 22)
   What: Not be subject to automated decisions affecting you
   Status: We do NOT use automated profiling for eligibility
   Exception: Fraud detection (you can request manual review)

TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS:
   Email: privacy@travelrust.com
   Subject: "[RIGHT NAME] Request - [Your Email]"
   Include: Email address, name, and specific request
   We will: Verify identity and respond within 30 days
